The White Room

The White Room is a short horror game where you’re a volunteer subject in a medicine experiment.

You live in a white room for 30 days. Every day follows the same routine: observe the room, and decide whether something anomalous happened. If yes, report it through the door. If not, go back to bed, and the next day begins.

What Happened?

A murder has been committed, and the truth is hidden within the evidence. As the investigator, your task is simple yet challenging: examine witness statements, analyze official documents, and piece together the timeline to deduce the truth.

Read through conflicting witness statements and official records. Uncover motives, alibis, and secrets hidden between the lines. Identify who killed the victim and why.

Silent Canvas

You’re the new night guard of a quiet art gallery. Your job is simple: walk the halls, watch the cameras, and look at the paintings.

Every night, one thing may be wrong. A face that wasn’t smiling. A sound that shouldn’t be there. A picture that isn’t quite the one you remember. Or nothing at all.
